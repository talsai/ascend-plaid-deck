How Ascend verifies a claimant's bank account in seconds, confirms the account belongs to them, and sends only payment-ready accounts to disbursement, using Plaid Link, Auth and Identity Match.

Benefits Administration
Seconds, not 1–3 days

Most claimants are verified while they're still on the page, so payments go out the same day.

Program Integrity & Fraud Ops
The account is theirs

Every account is matched to the claimant's record before money moves. Ascend sets the thresholds.

Constituent Experience
Nobody is forced to log in

Bank login, typed numbers or a prepaid card. Every path ends with a clear next step.

      Plaid API
      • Link, Auth, Identity Match
      • Webhooks back to Ascend
